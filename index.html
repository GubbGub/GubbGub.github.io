AP Research In progress

Using localized, sensor-driven dynamic traffic signal phasing simulations to reduce vehicle conflict points and improve safety at blind-crest intersections with flashing yellow arrows

At a blind crest, drivers turning on a flashing yellow arrow can't see oncoming cars until it's almost too late. I'm simulating whether sensors that change the signal phasing in real time can cut down on those conflicts.

Runner Thermo

An AP Physics II lab, rebuilt as a Java app. It works out how much power a sprinter produces in horsepower, and flags stopwatch times that would beat Usain Bolt.

Un labo de physique (AP Physics II) transformé en application Java. Elle calcule la puissance d'un sprinteur en chevaux-vapeur et signale les chronos qui battraient Usain Bolt.

  • Java
  • Swing
  • jpackage
